# Request from the Head of Customer Success, Lumen Bikes (fictional)

Lumen Bikes rents e-bikes to commuters on a monthly subscription in three cities.
Cancellations went up last quarter and my team only hears about unhappy riders
when they cancel. I'd like the data team to "build a churn model" so my four
account managers know who to call each week.

What we have:
- Subscription records for the last two years: start date, plan, city, cancel date.
- Ride logs from the bikes: trips per day and distance.
- Support tickets, tagged by topic (battery, brakes, billing, other).

We do not track why people cancel; the cancel form has no reason field.
Each account manager can make about 25 calls a week.
